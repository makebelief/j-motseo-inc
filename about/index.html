THE PRACTICE

Tomorrow’s Lawyers Today.

Founded in 2023, J Motseo Inc. operates from Braamfontein in central Johannesburg. Its published practice profile emphasises rapid-response criminal defence and police bail, with a wider offering in corporate and commercial disputes, civil litigation, and labour law.

The firm serves individuals, families and businesses in Johannesburg and surrounding communities, adapting legal strategies to the issues and records in each matter.

Its Braamfontein location places it near key legal and commercial centres of Johannesburg.
